Meet Our Artists

Alex Carter

Specialty: Realism & Portrait

Alex has been tattooing for over 8 years, with a passion for bringing faces and detailed scenes to life on skin. He believes every tattoo should capture the essence of its subject.

Jamie Rivera

Specialty: Fine Line & Floral

Jamie specializes in delicate, intricate designs that flow with the body's natural lines. Her floral work is renowned for its elegance and attention to detail.

Ryan Cole

Specialty: Traditional & Japanese

Ryan brings bold, timeless designs to life with a modern twist. His traditional American and Japanese pieces are characterized by strong lines and vibrant colors.
