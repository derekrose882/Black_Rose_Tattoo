Frequently asked

Can't find what you're looking for? Give us a call at (555) 123-4567. We're happy to help.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome Tuesday through Saturday, depending on artist availability. Small pieces and flash designs are best suited to walk-ins; larger custom work is by appointment.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Our shop minimum is $100. Most custom pieces are priced by the hour (from $160/hr) or as a flat rate quoted at your consultation, so there are no surprises.

Do I need a deposit?

A $100 deposit secures your appointment and goes directly toward the cost of your tattoo. It is refundable with 48 hours’ notice.

How old do I need to be?

You must be 18 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID. No exceptions, even with parental consent.

How do I take care of a new tattoo?

Keep it clean, moisturized and out of direct sun for two weeks. We send every client home with a detailed aftercare guide and are always a message away.
